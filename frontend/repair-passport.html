GOOD TO KNOW

Before you book.

A few useful details to make your repair enquiry easier.

How much will my repair cost?+

Costs depend on your device, issue and required parts. Contact FixMate to discuss an estimate before agreeing to a repair.

How long will a repair take?+

Timing depends on the fault, parts availability and service requirements. Ask us for an estimate for your particular device.

Does submitting this form confirm my booking?+

No. This form opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. Your request is sent only when you press Send in WhatsApp, and a booking is not confirmed until the details are agreed.
